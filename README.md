# Head Honchos Hair — NOIR Concept Website

An independent dark-luxury website concept for Head Honchos Hair, 13–14 Meer Street, Stratford-upon-Avon.

## Preview
https://willoughbyorchard-cpu.github.io/head-honchos-noir-concept/

## What is included
- NOIR-inspired charcoal, cream and gold visual style
- Responsive layout and mobile navigation
- Scroll-triggered reveal animations, staggered cards, parallax hero and progress indicator
- Salon details and contact actions based on publicly listed information
- Service descriptions based on public directory descriptions

## Important before using commercially
This is an unsolicited, unofficial design concept and is not approved by Head Honchos Hair. Images are representative stock photos, not verified photos of the salon or its customers. Replace them with owner-approved images and verify opening hours, services, prices and contact details with the business before launch.
