Good to know

Before you
come in.

A few useful things to check before your visit. For the most accurate answers, contact the salon directly.

Call with a question ↗

How do I choose the right service?

Tell the team what you have in mind and ask which appointment is best for your hair goals. Service names and suitability should be confirmed directly with the salon.

How much will my appointment cost?

Prices can vary by service and stylist. Contact Head Honchos Hair for current prices before arranging your visit.

Can I ask about a colour change?

Yes — contact the salon to discuss your idea, whether a consultation is recommended and how much time to allow.

Where is the salon?
